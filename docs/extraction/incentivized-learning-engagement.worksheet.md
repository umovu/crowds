## Stage 2 — Chains

### C1 (from P1, P2)

> Student is at home with comfort and flexibility -> therefore values convenience and self-paced engagement -> therefore prefers online lectures that allow eating, casual dress, and own schedule -> therefore reads online learning as a positive, comfortable experience

### C2 (from P3, P4, P5, P6, P7)

> Student is at home and struggles with self-discipline or prefers social/physical presence -> therefore values structure, peer interaction, and immediate feedback -> therefore dislikes online learning and finds it difficult -> therefore reads online learning as an inferior, isolating experience

### C3 (from P8, P9, P10, P11, P12, P13)

> Student participates in gamified quiz (Mentimeter) during class -> therefore evaluates engagement through interactivity, anonymity, and peer comparison -> therefore behaviour ranges from active learning to social loafing or superficial answering -> therefore reads gamified e-learning as engaging but with risks of disengagement and cheating

### C4 (from P14, P15, P16, P17, P18, P19, P20)

> Student is exposed to public leaderboards or real-name displays -> therefore evaluates self-worth through social comparison and fear of embarrassment -> therefore avoids participation or feels demotivated unless anonymity is preserved -> therefore reads gamified reward systems as pressuring and potentially demotivating

### C5 (from P21, P22, P23, P24, P25, P26, P27, P28, P29, P30, P31, P32, P33, P34, P35)

> Learner or parent experiences school reward ceremonies based on win/loss history -> therefore evaluates fairness, inclusion, and motivational value of public rewards -> therefore supports or opposes public ceremonies and prefers individualised recognition -> therefore reads visible reward policies as either motivating or exclusionary and unfair
