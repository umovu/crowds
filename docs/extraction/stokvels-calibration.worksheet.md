## Stage 2 — Chains

### C1 (from P1, P2)

> unemployed woman with limited household income -> therefore stokvel is evaluated as a tool to separate and time-shift spending (groceries vs. children's clothes/school fees) -> therefore joins and contributes to a grocery stokvel -> therefore the stokvel product is read as a disciplined, collective savings mechanism that smooths consumption across the year

### C2 (from P4, P13, P14)

> witnessing neighbours' suffering during funerals -> therefore mutual support is evaluated as a necessary community response -> therefore forms or joins a burial society, pooling resources for funeral equipment and labour -> therefore the stokvel product is read as a community-based insurance and mutual-aid institution for crisis events

### C3 (from P6, P7, P8, P9)

> desire to acquire household assets and make lump-sum investments -> therefore stokvel is evaluated as a forced-savings device that converts small regular contributions into large periodic payouts -> therefore participates in rotating savings schemes to buy furniture, building materials, appliances, or pay school fees -> therefore the stokvel product is read as an informal savings-and-investment vehicle that enables asset accumulation

### C4 (from P10, P15, P16, P17)

> need for accessible credit and social connection -> therefore stokvel is evaluated as a trusted circle where members can borrow and lend with interest, and share problems and ideas -> therefore engages in lending to members and trusted outsiders, and uses meetings for socialising and planning new ventures -> therefore the stokvel product is read as a micro-credit platform and social-capital network

### C5 (from P3)

> exposure to stokvel members (visible uniforms) and personal networks -> therefore stokvel is evaluated as an accessible, socially endorsed institution with clear entry rules -> therefore asks about joining procedures and pays the joining fee -> therefore the stokvel product is read as a membership-based organisation spread through social learning and peer recruitment
